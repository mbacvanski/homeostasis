A circular Braitenberg-style agent (radius 0.5) in a 15×15 box, simulated LIVE — press Play. Two rim sensors at ±45° read wall proximity, two effectors are wheel speeds, and the reservoir between them is the same homeostatic core as the other case studies (input weights 4, per the released code). Touching a wall clamps the position, kicks the heading ±45° at random, and scores a hit — the case study's outcome measure. The perturb variant is the published run: from step 1001 the sensors are swapped and doubled — watch the hit burst, then the recovery as the homeostatic rule re-adapts. Click (or drag) anywhere in the arena to teleport the agent — the H28c displacement probe by hand: drop a settled agent wall-adjacent and watch it drift back to its groove. The flank-champion variant is a tracking run and lives in the batch section below.
