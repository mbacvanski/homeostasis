A circular Braitenberg-style agent (radius 0.5) in a 15×15 box: two rim sensors at ±45° read wall proximity, two effectors are wheel speeds, and the reservoir between them is the same homeostatic core as the other case studies (input weights 4, per the released code). Touching a wall clamps the position, kicks the heading ±45° at random, and scores a hit — the case study's outcome measure. The perturb variant is the published run: from step 1001 the sensors are swapped and doubled, and the hit burst then recovery shows re-adaptation. The fifth variant, flank-champion, is a different animal — an evolved edge-holder: selection aims where homeostasis cannot. It runs the tracking task with the H30-evolved config, which was selected for holding |heading error| in the [50, 90]° band; on its good seeds (try 5, then 4) the agent orbits the stimulus 65–90° off-center forever — a set-point no homeostatic comfort condition expresses. It is seed-brittle (evolved on 3 random seeds per generation): other seeds never latch.
