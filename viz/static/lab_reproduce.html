structure-only heredity fails three ways; only full-state budding cascades — you can't inherit a lock.

Whether a fresh follower ever acquires the pacemaker's orbit is a lottery over wiring and early state (H87/H88). H97 asked if reproduction at comfort beats it: a follower that has held lock (dist < 4.8 to the blind wall-circling pacemaker) for 1,200 consecutive steps spawns one offspring at its own position — one spawn per agent, population cap 6, everyone sensing the pacemaker exclusively. All machinery is imported from the H97 script, never re-implemented: the co-simulation loop replayed here is bit-identical to the campaign run. The four rungs add inheritance cumulatively. 1 · position only — the child gets the parent's in-basin position but fresh wiring and a mutated genome: it loses the competence lottery and fails. 2 · + wiring heredity — the parent's exact wiring seed too: still fails, because it starts cold (the acquisition lottery). 3 · pure clone — genome and wiring bit-identical, mutation exonerated: still fails, so the barrier is internal state. 4 · budding — copy the parent's entire dynamical state (activations x, targets T, weights W, spike buffers, heading): the child is born mid-lock, and spawns cascade until the population fills its cap with every agent locked. The heritable unit is the entire dynamical state, not the structure — you can't inherit a lock.
