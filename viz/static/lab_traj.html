One run of the closed-loop tracking task, stepped exactly as the lab campaign runs it (scripts/lab/common.py run_closed_loop: sense → step → act → advance). These agents follow by velocity entrainment: the loop locks the agent's turning rate to the stimulus's angular velocity, with no position servo — the mean signed turn per degree of heading error is ≈ 0 — so the heading rides parallel to the stimulus and drifts within (and out of) the ±45° band rather than being pulled to its center. The optional effector swap inverts the motor mapping mid-run; the segment strip shows whether tracking recovers. The noise slider adds per-step uniform ±σ sensor noise (the H51 arm): on statue03 — the under-plastic regime that starves itself in sensory dark states — σ = 0.1 rescues tracking (16-seed mean 0.367 → 0.533) by keeping input flowing, while σ = 0.2 washes out the contrast it restored.
