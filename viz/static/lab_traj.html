One run of the closed-loop tracking task, stepped exactly as the lab campaign runs it (scripts/lab/common.py run_closed_loop: sense → step → act → advance). These agents follow by velocity entrainment: the loop locks the agent's turning rate to the stimulus's angular velocity, with no position servo — the mean signed turn per degree of heading error is ≈ 0 — so the heading rides parallel to the stimulus and drifts within (and out of) the ±45° band rather than being pulled to its center. The optional effector swap inverts the motor mapping mid-run; the segment strip shows whether tracking recovers.
