At the midpoint of a tracking run on the ridge config (leak .25, wlr .1, tlr .01) a fraction of the reservoir's nodes is destroyed outright, and the same wound is run twice: learning on (arm kill-mid) versus learning frozen at the kill (kill-mid-frozen), both stepped by scripts/lab/common.py run_closed_loop — the H53 campaign's exact runs. Physiology always repairs: the kill halves the survivors' recurrent drive, errors go negative, and the integral controller re-inflates surviving weights (watch w̄ climb — deafferentation-induced synaptic scaling from eq. 5 alone) until the spike rate f returns near its pre-kill level, while the frozen arm's activity stays collapsed. Behavior is buffered by redundancy: at a 10% kill the frozen arm survives fine (repair's transient churn can even cost more than the wound); at 50% the frozen arm loses badly and only the learning arm claws its score back.
