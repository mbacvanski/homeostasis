The pursuit task joins the family's parts: the wall-avoidance body (differential drive, invisible walls that clamp and kick) with a tracking-style retina aimed at a moving stimulus — here a single full-circle eye of 91 sensors — whose activation falls off with distance, so approach feeds the network and escape starves it. Two stored genomes: the H34 orbital champion (a genome+seed PAIR, wiring seed 66777, N=64; evolved to minimize late distance on orbit — phase-locked pursuit that collapses on waypoint motion), and the H55 blind sweeper (the ballistic GA's champion: evolved below the re-lock horizon, where vision has zero marginal fitness, its input_weight sits at the range floor — it cruises regardless of the stimulus). On ballistic motion targets spawn on an edge, fly straight, and respawn on exit; a catch is closing within 1.5 units during a crossing. The H55b story to verify by eye: at speed 0.15 (~70-step crossings) both genomes sit near the blind-body chance floor ≈0.2 — each crossing ends before entrainment can lock — while at 0.04 (~275-step crossings, past the 90–225-step re-lock horizon) the H34 champion catches over half the crossings and the sweeper still just cruises. Slow speeds leave only ~13 crossings in a 3600-step run, so give the catch rate 7200+ steps to settle.
