Two stored genomes: the H34 orbital champion (a genome+seed PAIR, wiring seed 66777, N=64; evolved on orbit — watch it settle into phase-locked pursuit inside the target's ring, and collapse on waypoint motion), and the H55 blind sweeper (evolved below the re-lock horizon; its input_weight sits at the range floor — it cruises regardless of the stimulus). On ballistic motion targets spawn on an edge, fly straight, and respawn on exit; a catch is closing within 1.5 units during a crossing.
