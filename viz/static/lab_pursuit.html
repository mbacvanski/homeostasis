The pursuit task joins the family's parts: the wall-avoidance body (differential drive, invisible walls that clamp and kick) with a tracking-style retina aimed at a moving stimulus — here a single full-circle eye of 91 sensors — whose activation falls off with distance, so approach feeds the network and escape starves it. Both presets run the H34 jointly-evolved champion pair (genome + wiring seed 66777, N=64; selection minimized late distance on the orbit stimulus). perfect-pursuer: the champion on its evolved motion. pursuit-fails: the same champion, same wiring, with the stimulus switched to unpredictable waypoint motion. The pair is also wiring-brittle: try a fresh seed and the genome lottery mostly fails even on orbit.
