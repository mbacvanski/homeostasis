One homeostatic node under constant drive — the paper's rule with everything else stripped away. Each step the node leaks a fraction leak of its activation, integrates a constant input μ, spikes when x ≥ ρ·T (the threshold is subtracted on spiking), and then nudges its target by target_lr·E, where E = x′ − T and T never falls below the floor Tfloor = 1. Every run ends in one of four states: dead-floor (silent and permanently uncomfortable), silent-comf (T adapts onto the leak balance point x* = μ/leak and the error vanishes), spiking (sustained firing, cycle-mean E ≈ 0), or frozen-cycle (a mode-locked spike pattern whose discomfort never resolves). Two laws organize the map: the comfort split at μ = leak·Tfloor separates dead-floor from silent-comf, and on the spiking branch the duty law f = (μ/T − leak)/ρ pins the firing rate — compare observed f against predicted f in the strip below. Initial conditions matter: a cold start (x₀ = 0) and a hot start (x₀ = 3μ/leak) can land in different basins — try the bistable preset with the hot checkbox on and off.
