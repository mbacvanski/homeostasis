Three Laws and a Ridge

Why homeostatic reservoir networks work, where they work, and what they are actually doing — an overnight preregistered investigation (~28,000 runs, 99 hypotheses H1–H99, every number re-derivable from scripts/out/lab/; running log in scripts/lab/LEDGER.md, synthesis in docs/design_space.md, interactive verification at /lab in the repo visualizer).

The model: leaky integrate-and-fire nodes, each keeping its own activation near a target by two local rules — weights of recently-active afferents absorb the error (eq. 5), and the target itself drifts toward comfort (eq. 4). No reward, no objective. The campaign asked: what do these rules actually do, which design parameters matter, and what kind of controller emerges when the loop closes through a body?

The three exact laws

Law 1 · comfort split

A node under stationary drive μ ends silent-comfortable (target T → μ/leak, error → 0) iff μ ≥ leak·T_floor, and dead at the floor below. Verified 674/674 cells. Spiking onset from a cold start sits at μ ≈ ρ·leak·T₀ (86%; the residue is the race between activation rising and the threshold chasing it).

Law 2 · duty law

Where spiking persists, the spike fraction is f = (μ/T − leak)/ρ with zero free parameters — median residual 0.0000 on 419 single-node cells, and corr +0.999 per (node, window) inside the full recurrent network in its most violent regime. Activity is fully slaved to (drive, target); all dynamics live in how W moves μ and how T moves.

Law 3 · gated integral controller

Each node's total incoming weight changes by exactly −weight_lr·E per step when any afferent spiked, else not at all (verified to 1e−14 in closed loop). Eq. 5, for all its per-synapse bookkeeping, is a pure drive servo. The reduced model is therefore derived, not fitted: two coupled integral controllers per node — fast drive servo (wlr), slow set-point servo (tlr) — sharing one spike-gating fabric. Its predicted absorption corner P* ≈ 2π/(wlr·f̄) ≈ 420 steps matches the measured 240–960.

The ridge: where tracking lives

The historic 241-config sweep never varied weight_lr — and that turns out to be the axis. wlr = 0 is the statue (targets can only relabel drive as comfortable, so the network sits at f ≈ 1.0, blind); wlr ≥ 3 explodes numerically; and in between, one parameter change from the 2024 defaults (weight_lr 1.0 → 0.1, the 2021 paper's value) doubles tracking and removes the wiring lottery (0.633, 12/12 seeds ≥ 0.35, vs 0.325, 4/12).

Why a ridge? Signal-to-noise. Reconstructing stimulus position from spikes under sinusoidal slip: the ridge regime carries 3–10× more stimulus information than anything else probed. Below it, saturation destroys selectivity; above it, the servo's own churn buries the signal (at 2024 defaults, activity sits at f ≈ 0.3–0.4 for every input schedule, stationary included — input-powered, but input-statistics-independent).

What the agent actually is: entrainment, not servo control

A hand-written P-controller on the same 62 sensors scores 0.999; a 1-step flow-greedy controller 0.898 (flow-maximization ≈ centering in this embodiment — the input-flow thesis's geometric basis, measured). The best homeostatic config (w1′) reaches 0.82 — but not by doing worse P-control:

  • It follows without position feedback. Velocity-follow ratio 0.84 at w1′ (0.98 late), through every reversal — while the signed response to heading error is +0.0006 (zero) or even −0.12 (anti-corrective). There is no servo inside.
  • It is swap-immune. Invert the effectors mid-run and performance recovers within one segment everywhere (ratios 0.93–1.03) — and the wiring's innate reflex kernel does not predict per-seed success (sign agreement 0.44). Nothing about "which wire turns left" matters for long.
  • The ratchet pawl: out of view the agent goes still (RMS turn 0.17 vs 1.20 in view; dark-stall 93% on the ridge) and the periodic world returns the stimulus to it. Periodicity is load-bearing, exactly as the earlier toy-world impossibility result predicted.

The mechanism, assembled: slip passes the plasticity bandpass and drives spikes; spikes drive turning; the turn bias is the slow learned variable dragged along by the stimulus. Tracking is velocity entrainment with a flow ratchet. The paper's phrase "Gibsonian resonance" turns out to be mechanically accurate — and the error-correcting-servo reading is wrong.

Where the bias lives: decomposing the output pools' duty difference (which is the turn command, corr +1.000) into input vs recurrent components: at w1′ the bias is carried by the recurrent drive (+0.37 with stimulus direction; input pathway nil) — genuinely written into W by the servo and rewritten at each reversal. The lag-servo alternative is dead: heading error keeps a constant +7–9° offset in both directions. At ridge25 the carrier is mixed — even the bias's storage medium is a place in design space.

And the bandpass is in activity, not information: at slip period P=8 (~16°/step) activity collapses to f = 0.003 while reconstruction gain rises to 0.505 — fast slip sparsifies spiking into hyper-selective events. Absorption kills slow signals' activity and information together; fast signals lose activity but keep information.

The channels compete for the same error: fast targets absorb E before the weights can act (protecting the very gain the weights should erode), which is visible as the dead right-hand column of the A1 plane. And the target channel's role resolved cleanly: freezing T at its evolved heterogeneous per-node values costs nothing (0.789 vs full 0.791) while homogeneous T=1 costs ~0.09 — targets are a calibration channel that builds a static per-node dynamic-range profile and can then stop; weights are the computation channel and must keep running.

The night extensions: three new arenas wall replicated · pursuit bounded · Pong resolved

Case study 3 (wall avoidance) — implemented, replicated, and it flips the sign. The paper's third task now exists in the repo (all five qualitative anchors pass: collisions 0.168 → 0.004/step, 96% of seeds collision-free late, constant-direction circling, learning-off bounces forever, inversion re-stabilizes). Across a 91-config screen the flow–success correlation is −0.86 among living configs (vs +0.77 tracking, +0.95 Pong) with flow stability at +0.88 — the input-flow thesis's preregistered counterexample confirmed: the body decides the sign. Two structural findings: this is the family's only task solvable by death (40/91 dead configs score perfectly by never moving), and stabilization is stability selection, not punishment learning — collisions are nearly invisible to the network (1.04× baseline plasticity; the saturated statue is literally deaf to them), and a settled agent teleported wall-adjacent drifts back to flat sensory territory.

Pong at 48-seed cluster resolution — channel competition is signed. The published cell reproduces exactly (0.600±0.111, second bit-identical cross-machine check). wlr 0.3 / tlr 0.01 beats it at 0.658; but (wlr 1.0, tlr 0.01) collapses to 0.346: in Pong's subcritical init the fast targets are a damper on weight-growth overshoot, not a competitor. Supercritical → targets harm; subcritical → targets stabilize. One learning-rate change improves all three published case-study configs.

Pursuit (ladder rung 3) — the family's competence boundary, fully mapped. A new task (tracking's retina on the wall-avoidance body, moving stimulus) that a P-controller solves near-perfectly defeats every hand-designed configuration through five theory-guided rescue rounds (absorbing dead basin in space; wall-pinned dark poses; churn-forced cruise that abolishes the ratchet's waiting state). But joint (genome, wiring) evolution finds a perfect pursuer in three generations — and the autopsy is the campaign's most beautiful exhibit:

And the boundary resolves into a law. The perfect pursuer scores 0.12 on unpredictable motion and 0.02 on a perfectly predictable straight-line shuttle; reward-scaled homeostasis does nothing; one-step reward-directed three-factor credit does nothing (shuffled-reward control identical). Evolving against other shapes: a 4:1 ellipse and the shuttle both yield toll-booths — champions that park at a favorable point (the shuttle's at speed exactly 0.000) and let the passing target pay them proximity credit — while a near-circular ellipse (curvature ratio 1.27) evolves a genuine velocity-matched follower (near-target 1.00, dist 0.58, agent speed 0.159 ≈ the target's 0.15).

The constant-control law: entrainment reaches exactly those motions whose co-moving frame can be held with ~constant control — constant curvature means a constant turn rate, so the control signal is itself stationary and absorbable; past the tolerance (measured: ratio 1.6–2.5, about 2× control modulation, with a soft edge), selection substitutes stillness at favorable points; without periodicity, nothing. This is the quantitative form of the mentors' ladder rung 3 and of the prediction-vs-Gibsonian question: within the law's reach the baseball intuition is exactly right — no prediction needed. Beyond it, nothing tested crosses, and the live question is what minimal structural addition buys generality.

And the entrainment model closed: open-loop synaptic rewrite takes 15–60 steps at every wlr while closed-loop re-locking takes 90–225 — the body, not the synapse, is the slow variable. A 2-ODE model with both parameters derived from measurements (and the aperture ratchet as geometry) predicts re-entrainment times for 3/4 regimes within ~15%, including the counterintuitive slowness at high wlr.

The two-body problem, and the band clause. Two mutually-tracking homeostats collapse to collective stillness (a static partner is already a stationary stimulus); churn pairs chase without locking; a mixed pair shows the churner animating the absorber but not leading it — churn motion is aperiodic, the forbidden class. And the pacemaker test closes the loop on the family's own artifacts: a recorded wall-circler orbit is unfollowable at its native 6.4°/step yet perfectly followed at 2.1°/step (near-target 1.00 by generation 5). The competence law is two-clause: constant-control shape (tolerance ≈ 2× curvature modulation) AND frame rate inside the entrainment band. Ecology corollary: collective entrainment needs a slow periodic pacemaker. Also measured: the wiring lottery lives in the frozen adjacency (no weight-space operation fixes it: 0/16), a minimal grow/prune structural rule is the first within-life mechanism to cross it (3/16), lifelong rewiring destroys solved tasks, and a developmental window — structural exploration that closes — captures the benefit at zero cost. The family, minimally extended, rediscovers why development exists.

And scaling is inert. A cluster N-line (N = 200 → 2000, in-degree and input wiring pinned, 24 seeds/cell): the ridge does not move, scores show no trend (N=2000 is nominally the best cell), internal coordinates are constant across the 10× range, and the duty law holds at N=2000 (corr 0.988). The organizing variables of this family are degrees and rates, not counts.

Night two: robustness, the third clause, and the static cliff H51–H99

Noise is a resource for the under-plastic — via the loop, not the reservoir. Uniform sensor noise σ=0.1 rescues the statue regime (wlr 0.03: score 0.367 → 0.533, reliability 0.44 → 0.81) and even lifts wlr 1.0; σ=0.2 hurts everywhere. The mechanism is not desaturation — open-loop, σ=0.1 changes nothing (reconstruction gain 0.001) while σ=0.2 does desaturate. It is a dark-trap escape: the failing agent spends 35% of steps in darkness, where zero input flow means zero learning signal, an absorbing sensory dead state. σ=0.1 abolishes darkness (input duty 0.65 → 1.00) while barely touching the motor; σ=0.2 keeps duty at 1.00 but washes out contrast. The optimum sits exactly where darkness is abolished and contrast survives.

Node death: the physiology always repairs; behavior only needs it for big wounds. Kill 10–50% of nodes mid-run (true adjacency removal — plasticity cannot regrow it). Spike rate recovers under learning and stays collapsed frozen; the repair channel is Law 3 itself: the kill halves each survivor's total input, the error goes persistently negative, and the integral controller re-inflates surviving weights (w̄ 0.106 → 0.210 at 50% kill — a near-exact 2× for the halved in-degree). This is Turrigiano-style compensatory synaptic scaling, from eq. 5 with nothing added. Behaviorally though, redundancy buffers: a frozen network shrugs off 10% death entirely, and at small wounds the repair transient is net harmful (the inflammation costs more than the injury) — repair pays only at 50% kill (median 0.432 vs 0.295), though learning abolishes the catastrophic tail even at 10% (0/16 dead vs 3/16).

Sparsity relocates the starting point — and the conservation law is exact. With input and output wiring pinned, total recurrent input after adaptation is conserved across a 40× density range (w̄·p·N = 2.01–2.17; recurrent gain 0.93–1.02): criticality is wiring-invariant. Performance at the ridge is flat — but at wlr 0.03, p=0.02 scores 0.566 with 15/16 reliability at 16 seeds, the best tracking cell of the campaign (48-seed cluster calibration: 0.509/0.79 — still +0.05 and +0.18 reliability over the dense pair, with a third bit-identical cross-machine check): 4-link columns start at g≈1.5 instead of 7.5, so nearly-frozen plasticity suffices and churn never enters. Sparse wiring is pre-adapted. Density narrows the viable plasticity window instead of shifting it (at p=0.8, wlr 0.3 and 1.0 both collapse to 0/16 — one by a carrier-level loss with physiology fully renormalized, one by runaway potentiation); sparse is forgiving, dense is brittle. And it transfers: on Pong (mean-zero weights — there the controller grows input to comfort, the mirror image of tracking's erosion) the best cell is again sparse+slow (12-seed screen 0.679; at the paper's own 500-run scale with targets off: 0.610 ± 0.204 vs published 0.582 ± 0.100, z = +2.8), running nearly silent at f≈0.02. The best Pong players use about ten active neurons in five hundred.

The baseball test, run forward — and the competence law's third clause. New ballistic mode: targets fly straight through the arena. A constant-bearing approach is constant-control, so the law seems to promise interception — yet a 10-generation GA stays at the blind-body chance level. The timescales say why: crossings last ~70 steps, re-entrainment takes 90–225 (the body-clock measurement) — each encounter ends before a lock can form. Slow the targets and skill appears on schedule: the orbital champion's edge over blind grows +0.03 → +0.21 → +0.31 as crossings stretch past the horizon (catch 0.535 while blind falls). Clause (iii): the engagement must outlast the re-lock horizon — periodicity is just the easiest way to satisfy it forever. The evolutionary footnote: the GA champion bred below the horizon is numerically identical to its own blinded control — selection drove input weight to the floor and evolved a blind sweeper. Vision without lockable engagements has zero marginal fitness.

What aperiodicity actually forbids — three failed predictions that narrowed the truth. Slowing aperiodic waypoint legs past the horizon does not produce following; worse, the sighted champion (0.17) loses to a parked blind statue (0.43) that the loitering target keeps visiting. It is not the kinks: randomized mid-flight ±90° redirects leave interception untouched (+0.007, n=422, placebo clean; an earlier hazard-classified design was caught as duration-confounded by its own preregistered blind control). At matched engagement length, traversal works (+0.21) and loitering fails (+0.00): a through-flying target offers a monotone looming ramp the flow-ratchet can climb — the champion catches a third of crossings inside 100 steps; the blind control, none — while a loitering target flutters at mid-range and offers no ramp. And the harm mechanism is measured (H61): entrained turning is not an internal motor program but a closed-loop product of stable bearing geometry — the true champion turns 6.14°/step on orbit with its bearing pinned and 0.00°/step (median, two-sided) under loitering flow, where fluttering bearings cancel and only mean forward throttle survives. It cruises near-straight, ends wall-pinned, far from where the target loiters, while a parked blind statue wins passively. Flow is a throttle; stable geometry is the steering — vision under rampless flow unparks the agent without aiming it.

The reward ladder, completed — the barrier is task-side. Eligibility traces (two decay constants) sit exactly at the η=0 baseline, like reward-scaling and one-step three-factor credit before them. The decisive arm: traces with the homeostatic channel switched off mid-run — so nothing could erode what reward installs — also does nothing. Neither credit delay nor absorbability was the blocker; the task the ladder was aimed at (waypoint pursuit) violates the competence law's own clauses, so there is no gradient any weight rule could climb. Reward = selector, not shaper, now with its mechanism.

And the laws compose into a predictor — which exposed a cliff. Freeze both channels and cold-start network fate is computable from the wiring file alone: iterate the per-node duty law gated by the Law-1 reachability condition (drive ≥ leak·ρ·T). Result: r = 1.0000, median error 0.0% over 72 frozen runs (the two bugs on the way each turned out to be a missing law — the branch clip, then the reachability gate). But inverting it to design an interior rate fails constructively: predicted f is discontinuous — homogeneous frozen networks have no stable interior rates at all, only dead and saturated. Every competent behavior in this campaign lives at f ≈ 0.1–0.3: a state that exists only as the homeostat's dynamically maintained equilibrium. Adaptation holds the network on the cliff face — and manufactures the heterogeneous per-node target profile that, once frozen, is the only static design that works. Self-organized marginality, made precise.

And a second wind of overturned expectations (H69–H78). Long-horizon runs sag everywhere (−0.109 over 21,600 steps at the ridge, t=−3.6, n=48). The wander's bad segments are dark excursions (r(segment score, segment duty) = +0.52–0.81 — the noise-rescue trap, visited transiently by healthy agents), but the sag is not accumulating darkness — and it is not plasticity churn either: freezing everything makes it worse (lock decay is metastable, and the weight channel is the re-locking mechanism). The culprit is the target channel: freezing T at birth beats full homeostasis by +0.115 (t=+3.24, 48 seeds), and even the first 3,600 steps of target adaptation do lasting damage (+0.090, t=+2.65) — on the flagship task at its best operating point, eq. 4, the family's namesake set-point mechanism, is a pure liability (its harmful component boxed by three preregistered nulls: not the mean, not anatomically-selective, not activity-selective). The competence law's band clause got its full psychometric (lock plateau at target speeds 0.10–0.26 units/step; the low edge is jointly speed and speed-regularity). Information-per-spike is now in units: wiring density buys code sparsity buys ~11× bits/spike, and dense-quiet networks carry more information absolutely at a third of the spikes — the sparse-wiring advantage is dynamical, never informational. And the DNA question got its first generative answer: a GA over wiring-statistics generators (density, degree regularity, input-targeting bias, reciprocity) improves fresh-wiring reliability only +0.08 over Bernoulli at the ridge — topology statistics beyond density are nearly inert, though selection did keep inventing anti-hub input targeting (a quasi-feedforward sensory layer). And the closing boundary: there is no figure-ground. Give the proven follower two visible pacemakers — even one it cannot follow anyway — and it locks nothing (1.000 alone → 0.034 with the second bump; a distractor at 10% salience already abolishes the lock). Superposed flows leave no constant-control co-moving frame, and the architecture has no source separation. The depth-4 chain worked only because each link sees exclusively its own target — that sensing restriction was load-bearing. With the ~1% jitter tolerance, a knife-edge theme closes the campaign: perfect locks, self-repair, and N-invariance inside the single-source clean-flow manifold; collapse at every measured edge. Selective attention, not prediction, looks like the first structural addition a real multi-agent homeostatic ecology needs — and the campaign closed by building it (H82–H85b). Evolution over the genome space cannot find attention (GA plateau 0.21); a memoryless winner-take-all filter fails too (0.074 — selection flickers, teleporting the effective stimulus); winner-take-all with persistence restores the lock completely (1.000). In the full shared-visibility test — every agent seeing every other — sticky attention lets a follower lock a follower (a genuine all-visible chain), exposes one final emergent pathology (the follower seduces the leader: orbiting close, it becomes its leader's brightest source and captures its attention away from the pacemaker — naive salience inverts hierarchies), and a conservative switching threshold (5× for 300 steps) cures that as well: B holds the pacemaker at 1.000 while C holds B at 1.000, zero switches — the first stable all-visible homeostatic ecology, running on one latched selection bit per agent. A closing honesty note earned its own hypothesis number: the ridge's once-favored matched-timescale mechanism (the plasticity absorption corner meeting a behavioral period) was tested against the full 4,800-run plane and refuted with the wrong sign — crest wlr rises with duty rate (H94). The ridge's SNR character is measured; its exponent remains empirical and underived — the sharpest theory question the campaign leaves open. And the campaign's last measured boundary: entrainment acquisition is a fractal-like speckle in initial conditions (~10–25% per-attempt, unrescued by noise floors or slow-start curricula) — locks are near-permanent once formed, but forming one from arbitrary geometry is the rare event, which is why warm starts and selection are how every working setup here actually began. Two powered refinements close the record honestly: w1′'s exemption from the target-channel toxin is real (full homeostasis rises 0.819 → 0.880 over 21,600 steps) but its celebrated calibration benefit is transient-early only (+0.07 at 48 seeds, caught up to within noise by late); and with wander SD ≈ 0.29, early/late windows regress to the mean — the reversion-adjusted sags (full −0.102, freeze-at-birth +0.006) and same-window cross-arm contrasts on shared seeds are the load-bearing statistics throughout.

Scale calibrations, run last. At the paper's own 500-run scale, the best Pong cell (sparse wiring, slow weights, targets off) scores 0.610 ± 0.204 vs the published 0.582 ± 0.100 — a real (z = +2.8) but modest improvement bought at doubled variance; the stacked tracking config holds 0.512 / reliability 0.72 at 200 seeds. The pattern deserves its name: every effect in the campaign shrank monotonically from 12 → 48 → 200/500 seeds while keeping its sign — small-n screens find directions, large-n runs give the citable numbers. And the ridge's structure fell on the final pass (H98–H99): each run is a two-state lock process whose measured formation/destruction rates predict the whole (leak × wlr) plane at r = +0.878 (score = chance + locked-time × locked-quality), land the crest exactly at two of three tested leaks — and the destruction rate is already mechanistic: 91% of lock losses are dark-mediated (P(destroy | next segment dark) = 0.78 vs 0.29). The universal wander, the dark-excursion dips, and the crest are one unlock/relock alternation; what remains open is why view-keeping and dark re-acquisition depend on (leak, wlr) as they do.

Refuted along the way all preregistered

  1. "Homeostasis silences stationary input" — only the weight channel regulates; the statue is the target channel's own fixed point.
  2. "Spiking tracks stimulus statistics" at defaults — it is churn-set (the canonical prop_spiked ≈ 0.34 included).
  3. "The network self-excites" — darkness silences every regime.
  4. "The seed lottery is the innate kernel" — no; its microfoundation is open.
  5. "g_init organizes performance" — it organizes activity only.
  6. "Re-entrainment time ∝ 1/wlr" — higher wlr raises the ceiling but slows locking.
  7. "Bigger networks fail" — mostly a wiring confound: with in-degree and input wiring held fixed, N=100–800 is roughly stable (N=800: 0.536, 92% of seeds).
  8. "The seed lottery is a fixed wiring fate" — late segment scores within a run are mutually uncorrelated (+0.03): performance wanders, and much of the "lottery" is sampling noise of that wander. w1′'s 100%-seed reliability is a wander floor above threshold.

Open, ranked

  1. Traversal geometry, one level deeper: the loitering harm is now mechanistic (H61 — throttle without steering), but what makes a looming ramp sufficient for acquisition within 100 steps on fresh wirings is still unmodeled.
  2. What sets the wander itself — H66 showed it is universal (SD ≈ 0.26–0.28 in every cell tested; design moves the mean, and reliability is threshold-crossing statistics around it) — so what microdynamics generate that invariant?
  3. A speed regenerator for chains: the depth-4 ceiling turned out to be a speed floor (rings contract, co-rotation slows the target below the followable band) — can a link be designed to hold radius, where the heavy-wheel repeater failed?
  4. The dense-fast carrier loss (p=0.8, wlr=0.3: physiology fully renormalized, competence zero) — what structure in the weights dies when the same total is spread over 160 links?
  5. Formalize information-per-spike: the best Pong players run at f≈0.02 — a bits/spike curve is now clearly worth one clean experiment.
  6. Why does the bias carrier regionalize (pure W-storage at w1′, mixed on the ridge)?
